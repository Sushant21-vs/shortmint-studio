# ShortMint Studio v25 — Contact & Socials

## Update only
Replace `accounts.py`, `admin_backup.py`, `static/index.html`, and `static/dashboard.js`. Replace `static/shortmint-cloud-v24.js` and add `static/sidebar-contact-v25.css` and `static/sidebar-contact-v25.js`.

## Configure
Sign in as owner > Admin Panel > Public Profile · Contact & Socials. Add public email, phone number, Instagram profile/@handle, and up to 5 YouTube channels (one per line).

YouTube examples:
- `Main channel | https://www.youtube.com/@mychannel`
- `Second channel | https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCexample`

No private contact information is automatically published. Empty fields are not shown. Email opens mailto, phone opens the device dialer, social links open official sites.

## Caution
Render Free uses ephemeral SQLite storage. Before GitHub/Render redeployment download your owner backup. Backup/restore also covers these settings as part of the SQLite database. The cloud cursor now uses smoothed pointer tracking and cached particle sprites. No persistent database is added.
