SHORTMINT STUDIO V7 — PREMIUM CREATOR UI
=======================================
Made by Sushant Hero

THIS IS THE PREMIUM DESIGN UPGRADE, NOT A NEW BACKEND.
All v6 actions remain wired to the existing video converter, video downloader,
login/invite contact, credits and owner administration.

REPLACE ONLY 3 FILES ON YOUR EXISTING GITHUB REPOSITORY:
  static/index.html   (replace)
  static/premium.css  (add new)
  static/premium.js   (add new)

Your other files (app.py, accounts.py, downloads.py, app.js,
dashboard.js, dashboard.css, downloads.js, styles.css, render.yaml)
SHOULD REMAIN UNCHANGED. Keep the paths/folder structure exactly.

1) Download the ShortMint_v7_Premium_Replace_Only.zip package.
2) Extract the ZIP on your computer.
3) Open https://github.com/Sushant21-vs/shortmint-studio/tree/main/static
4) Replace index.html with the index.html inside the extracted static/ folder.
5) Upload premium.css and premium.js into the same static/ folder.
6) Commit your changes.
7) Render's auto-deploy (if enabled) should redeploy your website.
8) Open the live .onrender.com address, then hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R).

BEFORE STEP 4: IMPORTANT DATA WARNING
=====================================
Render FREE's local filesystem is temporary. A new deploy or container
restart can erase the SQLite account database, member credits, invite codes,
settings, and videos, even though you changed only front-end files.
Back up your data or arrange persistent storage before using this with paying
customers. UI improvements DO NOT fix this persistence problem.
