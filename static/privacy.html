Privacy information

ShortMint Studio · Account and creator services

ShortMint Studio collects only the information needed to provide accounts, credits, video editing, and support. The site owner is responsible for operating this service and complying with applicable privacy laws.

Information collected

For code-based registration, we store a username, password hash, login sessions, credit balances, promo/invite usage, credit purchase inquiries and project details. To limit new accounts to one per network address, we store a persistent hash derived from the sign-up IP address. IP-based restrictions can affect shared networks, and IP hashes can still be personal information. Older accounts may still have Google IDs/emails stored from the former sign-in system. If you send a purchase inquiry, the contact information you enter is shared with the site owner to respond.

What we do with it

We use account information to protect sign-in access, allocate welcome and promo credits, manage paid requests, prevent duplicated redemptions, and let you access your own ShortMint workspace. The owner can see member identities, account status, and credit history. We do not request access to your Gmail inbox or YouTube account.

Private orders and messages

When you submit an order enquiry or direct message, ShortMint stores your request, conversation replies, order status and timestamps in the account database. Only your signed-in account and the site owner can view that conversation. Messages remain in the account database after a normal browser refresh. Their availability depends on the owner maintaining persistent database storage; they are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers or sensitive documents through messages. The owner can manually review and respond, but a response time is not guaranteed.

Video files

Uploaded videos and generated MP4s are processed on the website hosting server. On the current free hosting plan they may be deleted without notice when the server restarts or redeploys. Back up finished work locally.

Optional speech tools

When you request automatic captions, the audio from your uploaded video may be sent securely to a third-party transcription service configured by the site owner; alternatively, the owner may run a local speech model. Do not submit private or sensitive speech without permission. Automatic captions are optional.

Storage and access

When the owner configures PostgreSQL, account data is stored with an external database provider instead of the temporary web server. Only authorized server routes can change credit balances. New accounts require a valid invite or promo code and a password. Google sign-in has been disabled. Previous Google-only users can recover their existing account by verifying the email originally linked to their Google login. For this purpose, ShortMint stores one-time verification-code hashes, code expiration and failed attempts, as well as temporary hashed IP/email rate-limit records. Verification emails are sent via an email delivery provider (Resend); your email address is shared with that provider only for sending the requested code. If you cannot access your original email, contact the owner for a separate identity-verification process. Access is restricted to the account owner and authorized site administrator.

Your choices

Contact the ShortMint owner using the contact details shown in the app to ask about the information associated with your account or request account removal. Do not upload sensitive documents or private recordings unless needed to create your Short.

Payments

ShortMint currently accepts purchase inquiries only. The website does not automatically charge your card or process a payment. Confirm payment arrangements directly with the owner.

This informational notice should be reviewed and customized by the website owner before public launch.
